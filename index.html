Before you go

What to bring

Comfortable shoes

Flat pedals fit most footwear, but closed-toe shoes are more comfortable over longer rides.

Water

Hubs don't sell drinks — guided tours exclude food and drinks too, so bring your own.

A rough route

Rentals are self-guided; check the pickup guide for hub hours before you set off.
