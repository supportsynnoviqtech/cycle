Rental Policies

Rules & Deposit Policy

Transparent rules built on full 24-hour rental days, clear deposit terms, and straightforward extensions.

Rental Duration

Full 24-hour rental blocks

Rentals are calculated in full 24-hour days from the time of pickup. Initial rental length ranges from 1 to 14 days.

Deposit Terms

refundable security deposit

A separate refundable security deposit of .00 per bicycle applies to all rentals. The deposit is held during your rental and refunded upon bicycle return in good condition.

Rental Extensions

Extension rules & pricing

Extension Length: 1 to 7 additional days (maximum 14 total days allowed per rental).

Tariff Rate: Extensions bill at the original daily tariff rate (/day City, /day Hybrid, /day Electric).

No Second Deposit: Extensions do NOT require a second deposit.

Tour Rules

Guided tour policies

Guided tours are fixed group prices ( Easy / Moderate) for 1 to 6 riders. No bicycle deposit is collected on guided tour bookings.

Operating Hours

Hub operating window

Hubs operate 08:00 – 20:00 UTC. Pickups must start between 08:00 and 19:59 UTC. Guided tour participants must arrive 15 minutes prior to departure.

Included Accessories

Equipment care & responsibility

Helmet Required

Helmet use is mandatory for all riders.

Locking Mandate

Bicycles must be locked when unattended.

Lighting

Use included front/rear lights when riding after dusk.

Honest Documents

Demonstration documents policy
